Caliz Music Studio

Caliz Music Studio, located in Angeles City, Pampanga, offers a space for worry-free jamming. It serves as a musical sanctuary for musicians and bands, and a welcoming place for singers and songwriters to enjoy music. The challenge was to bring the Caliz Music experience online through their website and digital brand elements.

To bring their physical vibe to life on the web, I focused on creating an immersive visual narrative. By combining a modern web layout with dark-mode aesthetic branding elements, the resulting digital experience accurately reflects the cool, creative sanctuary that their local music community enjoys, turning visitors into regular jamming patrons.
