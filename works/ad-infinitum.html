Ad Infinitum

An event organized by the University Student Council of HAU for the 2021 University Days of Holy Angel University. For decades, HAU has honed countless students in their future endeavors, encompassing years of holistic excellence, exceeding boundaries and capabilities, and showcasing utmost talent and creativity alongside camaraderie and unity.

As part of the creative team, the challenge was to establish a cohesive visual language that captured the energy and heritage of the university. This was achieved by designing dynamic motion graphics and social media content that engaged the student body across digital platforms, celebrating the spirit of the university days while pushing the boundaries of traditional campus branding.
