Wreck-It HAU

An event held by the university student council of HAU that shows how student youth advocates and show their skills and talents through different creations that empower and amplify the call for action towards different issues and concerns the world is facing. Here are few promotional materials that I made for this event entitled Wreck-It HAU.

The theme is based from the movie Wreck-It Ralph. The design showcases pixel art. The goal of this design is to create visually appealing products by the use of pixel generated graphics that convey certain messages or information. The logo, typography, social media frames, and posters focus on the overall principles of the theme.
