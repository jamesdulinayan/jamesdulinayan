Kaleidoscope

By buying or commissioning an artwork, a portion of the proceeds goes directly to the "HAUwak Kamay: Sabay Nating Abutin ang Pangarap na Edukasyon" donation drive. This initiative aims to assist Angelite students who lack the internet access needed to continue their education.

To showcase these creative pieces and inspire potential buyers, I edited a dynamic promo video showcasing the beautiful diverse submissions. The final video presentation leverages high-energy transitions, engaging motion typography, and sound design to elevate interest, turning artistic contributions into concrete student support.
